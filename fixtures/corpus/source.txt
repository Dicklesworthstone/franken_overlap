Before dawn, the observatory opened its copper shutters to a cloudless winter sky. The team measured a faint repeating signal, checked every instrument, repeated the calibration twice, and published the raw observations before proposing an explanation. Their report emphasized that the signal might still have an ordinary terrestrial source.

Three independent groups later reproduced the timing pattern from archived recordings. None claimed that the evidence established a new physical phenomenon, but all agreed that the anomaly deserved a controlled follow-up campaign.
